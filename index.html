From raw data to business value

Before a report, dashboard, analysis or AI use case can rely on data, several things have to happen behind the scenes.

01
↘

Sources

ERP and transactional systems, APIs, databases, files, SaaS and specialized systems can all contribute data.

02
⇄

Data is extracted and brought into the enterprise data environment through repeatable pipelines and processes.

03
⚙

Raw data is cleaned, standardized, modeled and prepared so it can support analytics and downstream use.

04
✓

Quality

Processes and checks help identify data issues and keep pipelines and data products reliable.

05

06

Trusted data becomes easier to discover, analyze and use in business decisions, reporting and AI.

THREE PARTS. ONE PURPOSE.

Different roles, shared purpose.

Our teams contribute at different points in the data lifecycle, but the goal is shared: make data useful and trustworthy.

01BUILD

Data Engineering

We build the data foundation: pipelines, integrations and transformations that make data available for use.

  • Build and maintain ELT pipelines
  • Integrate data from multiple systems
  • Transform and prepare data
  • Validate and monitor processes
  • Collaborate with business and analytics teams
02ENABLE

Data Services

We connect people, platforms, solutions and practices that enable data and analytics across the organization.

  • Requirements and data modeling
  • Data pipeline and platform support
  • Data maintenance and quality checks
  • Reporting and downstream enablement
  • User enablement and best practices
03TRUST

Metadata & Governance

We make data understandable and accountable through metadata curation, stewardship and governance.

  • Curate table- and column-level metadata
  • Establish ownership and accountability
  • Partner with business stewards
  • Support data catalog and discoverability
  • Enable governance throughout the data lifecycle

METADATA IN ACTION

How metadata becomes more useful

Enterprise Data Governance describes an AI-powered curation process in which AI accelerates metadata creation and stewards provide the business validation.

01

Steward input

Business context, glossaries and documentation provide the starting point.

02

AI curation

Metadata is generated at table and column level using the available context and profiling.

03

Validation

Automated checks help identify issues before metadata moves forward.

04

Steward review

Stewards validate, adjust and endorse the curated metadata before publication.

AI helps scale. Business expertise provides context.That combination helps make metadata more accurate, useful and trusted.
